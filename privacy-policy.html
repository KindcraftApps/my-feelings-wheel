Privacy Policy for My Feelings Wheel

Last updated: November 25, 2025

This privacy policy applies to the My Feelings Wheel app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Kindcraft Apps (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

Local Data Storage

The Application stores your feelings data locally on your device using encrypted storage. This data includes:

  • Feelings you log with the wheel interface
  • Comments and notes you add to feelings
  • App settings and preferences
  • Reminder settings

This data is stored only on your device and is encrypted. The Service Provider does not have access to this data, and it is not transmitted to any servers operated by the Service Provider.

Google Drive Backup (Optional Feature)

The Application offers an optional Google Drive backup feature. If you choose to use this feature:

  • You must sign in with your Google account
  • The Application will back up your encrypted feelings data to your personal Google Drive account
  • Data is stored in a hidden "appDataFolder" that only this Application can access
  • The Service Provider does NOT have access to your Google Drive or your backed-up data
  • Backups are automatic after logging feelings or changing settings (if you're signed in)
  • You can restore your data from Google Drive when reinstalling the app or using a new device

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

My Feelings Wheel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We only request the minimum Google Drive API scope necessary (appDataFolder)
  • Data is only used to provide backup and restore functionality
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising
  • We do not allow humans to read your data
  • We do not transfer your data to third parties (except as required by law)

Information We Do NOT Collect

The Application does NOT collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.) - except Google account email if you use Drive backup
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers for tracking
  • Usage analytics or statistics
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data

Third-Party Services

The Application uses the following third-party services:

Google Sign-In & Google Drive API

Used only for the optional backup feature. When you sign in:

  • Google provides your email address to identify your account (displayed in app settings)
  • The Application can read/write data to your Google Drive appDataFolder
  • You can revoke this access at any time through your Google Account permissions

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Security

The Service Provider is committed to protecting your data:

  • All feelings data is encrypted using industry-standard AES encryption
  • Encryption keys are stored securely on your device
  • When backed up to Google Drive, data remains encrypted
  • No data is transmitted to the Service Provider's servers (because we don't have any)

Your Rights and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Export: Export your feelings data as CSV at any time from app settings
  • Delete: Delete all feelings data from app settings
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the app removes all local data from your device
  • Revoke Google Drive access: Sign out in app settings or revoke access through Google Account settings
  • Delete Google Drive backup: Managed automatically when you revoke access or uninstall the app

Location Information

This Application does NOT collect, store, or use precise real-time location information from your mobile device.

Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. The Service Provider encourages all children to never submit any personally identifiable information through the Application. Parents and legal guardians should monitor their children's Internet usage and help enforce this Policy by instructing their children never to provide personally identifiable information through the Application without permission.

If you have reason to believe that a child has provided personally identifiable information to the Service Provider through the Application, please contact the Service Provider at kindcraftapps@gmail.com so that necessary actions can be taken.

You must be at least 16 years of age to consent to the processing of your personally identifiable information in your country (in some countries we may allow your parent or guardian to do so on your behalf).

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all use of the Application and delete all associated data by:

  • Deleting your feelings data from within the app settings
  • Signing out of Google Drive (if you use backup)
  • Uninstalling the Application using standard uninstall processes on your mobile device

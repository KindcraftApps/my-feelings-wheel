Privacy Policy for My Feelings Wheel

Last updated: November 25, 2025

This privacy policy applies to the My Feelings Wheel app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Kindcraft Apps (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Privacy First: The Service Provider does not collect, store, or have access to any of your personal data. All your feelings data stays on your device or in your personal Google Drive (if you choose to use the backup feature).

Google Integration: This app uses Google Sign-In and Google Drive API for optional backup functionality. No other Google data is accessed. The specific scope requested is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata, which is used only for storing encrypted backups in your personal Google Drive.

How Your Data is Stored

Local Storage (Default)

By default, all your data is stored locally on your device:

  • Feelings you log with the wheel interface
  • Comments and notes you add to feelings
  • App settings and preferences
  • Reminder settings

This data is encrypted and stored only on your device. The Service Provider has no servers, no databases, and no access to your data.

Google Drive Backup (Optional)

The Application offers an optional Google Drive backup feature that you can choose to enable:

  • You sign in with your own Google account
  • Your encrypted feelings data is backed up to your personal Google Drive
  • Data is stored in a hidden "appDataFolder" in your Drive that only this Application can access
  • Backups happen automatically after logging feelings or changing settings (while signed in)
  • You can restore your data from your Google Drive when reinstalling the app or using a new device

Important: The Service Provider does NOT have access to your Google Drive or your backed-up data. The data exists only in your personal Google account.

What the Service Provider Does NOT Collect

The Service Provider does NOT collect, store, transmit, or have access to:

  • Your feelings data
  • Your personal information (name, email, phone number, address, etc.)
  • Your location data
  • Device identifiers for tracking
  • Usage analytics or statistics
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data
  • Any personally identifiable information

Google Services Integration

Google Sign-In & Google Drive API

When you use the optional backup feature:

  • Google Sign-In allows you to authenticate with your Google account
  • Your email address is displayed in the app settings (stored only on your device)
  • The Application uses Google Drive API to read/write encrypted backup files to your personal Drive
  • Scope requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata (used only for encrypted backups in the hidden appDataFolder)

Revoking Google Access

You can revoke the Application's access to your Google account at any time:

  1. Through the app: Go to Settings → Sign out of Google Drive
  2. Through Google: Visit Google Account permissions and remove "My Feelings Wheel"

Revoking access will stop automatic backups but will not delete your existing backups from your Google Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

My Feelings Wheel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • The Application only requests the minimum Google Drive API scope necessary (appDataFolder access)
  • Google Drive access is only used to backup and restore your encrypted data to your own Drive
  • The Application does not use Google user data for serving ads
  • The Application does not share Google user data with third parties (except as required for backup functionality)
  • All data transferred through Google APIs is encrypted

Google's Privacy Policy: For information on how Google handles your data, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Security

Your data is protected through:

  • Encryption: All feelings data is encrypted using industry-standard AES encryption before storage
  • Local encryption keys: Encryption keys are stored securely on your device using Android's secure storage
  • End-to-end protection: When backed up to Google Drive, data remains encrypted
  • No remote servers: The Service Provider operates no servers that could be compromised

Your Complete Control

You have full ownership and control of your data:

  • View: Browse all your feelings in the journal section
  • Export: Export all your feelings data as CSV file at any time
  • Delete: Delete individual feelings or all data from app settings
  • Backup: Choose whether to enable Google Drive backup
  • Restore: Restore from Google Drive backup when needed
  • Revoke access: Sign out of Google Drive at any time to stop automatic backups
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the app removes all local data from your device

Children's Privacy

This Application is designed for personal use by individuals capable of providing informed consent. The Application does not target children and does not knowingly facilitate data collection from children under the age of 13.

Because all data is stored locally on the user's device (or in their personal Google Drive), the Service Provider has no way to determine the age of users and does not collect any personally identifiable information from users of any age.

Parents and legal guardians should monitor their children's app usage and Internet activity.

Location Information

This Application does NOT collect, request, store, or use location information from your mobile device.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated into this Application is:

  • Google Sign-In & Google Drive API: Used solely for the optional backup/restore feature

No other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.

Data Requests and Law Enforcement

In the event of a legal request or court order for user data:

  • The Service Provider cannot provide feelings data because it is not collected or stored by the Service Provider

  • The Service Provider has no servers, no databases, and no access to user data
  • The Service Provider cannot decrypt user data

Any legal requests would need to be directed to the individual user or to Google (for data in the user's Google Drive account).
